Read the journey as text

01 · Arrive

  1. Open the guest guide and see the welcome screen and door code.
  2. Tap Explore, view the English or Japanese address, and use Get directions to find the house.
  3. Check in from 3 PM using the displayed door code. Continue to settling in.

02 · Stay

  1. Connect to the Maana Kiyomizu Wi-Fi network; the Wi-Fi password is shared with your arrival details. Luggage assistance is also in Settle in: contact the team ahead of time about delivery to your next destination or Kyoto Station lockers.
  2. In How things work, find the room’s cooling or heating instructions, cleaning details, quiet hours after midnight, and house rules including no smoking and respect for neighbors.
  3. If you want plans, browse dining and experiences. Maana Atelier hosts Tama’s Cafe & Bar Pop-up on Saturday and Sunday, 15:00–21:00, alongside workshops. The pop-up serves Kyoto’s seasonal produce and beverages alongside the exhibition. Follow @kyoto.tama on Instagram for the weekend menu. Just come by: walk-ins only, with no reservations. Otherwise continue with your stay.
  4. Breakfast is at 8 AM, closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Contact the team about another time or a table.
  5. The guide says housekeeping is for stays of 2 or more nights, every 3 days between 11 AM and 1 PM. Request a skip or extra privacy if needed.

03 · Leave

  1. If you need luggage help, contact the team in advance. They can help arrange delivery to your next destination or Kyoto Station lockers.
  2. Leave towels in the bathroom. There is no need to strip the beds.
  3. Turn off all cooling and heating, close windows, and leave by 11 AM. Make sure the front door locks behind you.

Help

Email stay@maanahomes.com or visit the linked FAQ. Service hours are 10 AM–6 PM. The guide does not specify an after-hours route.
